What counts as a lead

You only pay for leads that meet every one of these. Anything that fails before delivery is never sent or charged.

A lead counts when it has

Real nameA genuine person's name, not a test, joke or made-up entry.
Email verifiedThe email address is well-formed and its domain can receive mail.
Phone verifiedThe number is checked with a phone verification service and is valid.
Postcode in your areaThe postcode is inside the service area you agreed to when the lead was delivered.
Your serviceThe enquiry matches the lead type you selected.
Not a duplicateThe same person hasn't been sent to you in the last 30 days.

What isn't covered

  • The homeowner doesn't answer.
  • The homeowner decides not to go ahead.
  • The homeowner gets other quotes.

If a lead doesn't meet the criteria

Open the lead in your account and choose Report a bad lead within 72 hours of delivery. Claims are checked automatically against the criteria above, and approved straight away when our checks confirm it. Otherwise, or if you'd rather email contact@quoteleads.com.au with the lead details, we review it and respond within 7 days.

An approved claim becomes credit for the lead's price, used automatically on your next leads. Full detail is in the Terms.
