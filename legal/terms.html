1. What you are buying

You buy leads one at a time, as they are delivered, for the lead types (for example Solar + Battery, Roofing) and the service area you choose. Your service area can be up to five suburbs with a radius of 50, 75 or 100 km around each, a list of postcodes, or a whole state. You can add or change lead types and areas in your account.

The price of each lead depends on its lead type and the pricing region your area falls in, and is shown before you pay, excluding GST. GST at 10% is added to every charge. We may change prices for future leads by updating them in your account. A change never applies to a lead already delivered.

Exclusive. Each lead is delivered to one business only. Where a homeowner's consent names your business, the lead can only go to you. We won't deliver a lead to you that was already delivered to another business. "Exclusive" doesn't mean the homeowner hasn't contacted, or won't contact, other businesses themselves.

2. What a lead is, and when it's billable

A lead is a new enquiry from a person who asked to be contacted about work matching your lead type, in your service area, and who agreed to be contacted by your business. A lead includes the details the person gave, such as name, phone, email, postcode and suburb, and project details, together with the consent they gave.

Before a lead is offered to you, we check that the phone number is valid with a phone verification service, that the email domain can receive mail, that the postcode is in your area, that it isn't a duplicate, and that it doesn't look fake. Leads that fail these checks aren't sent to you and aren't charged.

A lead is billable when it is delivered to you, even if the person doesn't answer, doesn't reply, changes their mind, isn't ready, chooses someone else, or doesn't go ahead. Leads that are invalid for the reasons in section 5 can be claimed back as credit.

3. Billing and payments

Your card. At checkout you save a card with our payment provider, Stripe, and pay for your first lead in advance. You authorise us to charge that card for each lead delivered to you after that, at its price plus GST, up to your weekly limit.

Charged before release. When a lead is matched to you, we charge your card (or use your account credit first, if you have any) before the lead's contact details are released to you. If the charge doesn't succeed, that lead is not delivered to you and you aren't charged for it.

Failed payments. If a charge is declined or your bank asks for extra verification, we pause delivery to your account straight away and send you a secure link by email and SMS to update your card. Delivery resumes automatically once a new card is saved and anything you owe is paid.

Invoices. We email you a weekly tax invoice for the previous week (Monday to Sunday, Sydney time), and every invoice can be downloaded from your account. Stripe may also email a receipt for each charge.

Chargebacks. If you dispute a charge with your bank, we may pause your account while the dispute is resolved. Please contact us first. Most issues can be fixed with a credit.

4. Weekly limit, pausing and areas

You choose a weekly limit for your account (currently between 5 and 50 leads). We will never deliver more leads to you in a week (Monday to Sunday, Sydney time) than your limit. Your maximum weekly spend is your limit multiplied by your highest lead price, plus GST, and is shown in your account. The limit is a maximum, not a promise of that many leads.

You can change your limit, pause or resume delivery, and pause or remove individual areas at any time in your account. Changes apply to leads matched after you make them. You aren't charged for anything while paused.

5. Bad-lead claims and credits

A lead counts (and is billable) when it has a real name, a verified email, a verified phone number, a postcode in your agreed area, matches the service you selected, and isn't a duplicate. See What counts as a lead.

You can claim a credit for a lead within 72 hours of delivery if it fails one of those criteria:

  • the phone number is invalid or disconnected;
  • the email address can't receive mail;
  • the postcode is outside your service area as it stood when the lead was delivered;
  • it doesn't match the service you selected;
  • it duplicates a lead delivered to you in the previous 30 days; or
  • it isn't a real person (for example a made-up name or a joke enquiry).

Not covered: the homeowner not answering, deciding not to go ahead, or getting other quotes.

Claim from the lead in your account. Claims are checked automatically against these criteria, and approved straight away where our checks confirm the reason. Otherwise, or if you email us at contact@quoteleads.com.au with the lead details, we review it and respond within 7 days. An approved claim becomes account credit equal to the lead's price excluding GST, and is used automatically on your next leads. Credits are not paid out as cash, except as required by law.

6. Delivery and your first lead

Leads are delivered in real time by email and SMS, to your CRM by webhook if you set one up, and to your account. Leads come from genuine homeowner demand in your area, so we can't promise a particular number in any week. Your first lead usually arrives within a few days, but it can take longer.

Your first lead. If the lead you paid for at sign-up hasn't been delivered within 7 days, we will refund it to your card. If we can't generate leads in an area you selected, we may pause that area, tell you, and refund your first lead if it hasn't been used.

7. Contacting your leads

Leads convert best when contacted within minutes. If you leave automatic follow-up switched on, we text each new lead on your behalf, in your business name, when it's delivered and again on days 2, 5 and 10 until they reply. Replies are answered automatically with the aim of arranging a call with you, and are shown in your account. Every message lets the person opt out, and follow-ups stop when they reply, opt out, or you mark the lead as contacted, quoted, won or lost. You can switch automatic follow-up off at any time.

8. Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate business details, including your ABN. Your business name is what homeowners see when they agree to be contacted, so changes are checked by us before they apply.
  • Contact leads lawfully, including under the Privacy Act 1988, the Spam Act 2003 and the Do Not Call Register Act 2006, and only about the enquiry they made.
  • Keep lead information secure, and don't sell, share or reuse it for other purposes.
  • Hold any licence, registration and insurance your work requires.
  • Keep your card, contact details and login secure and up to date.
