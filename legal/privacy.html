Privacy Policy

Version 24 November 2026 · QuoteLeads (ABN 83 894 208 171)

QuoteLeads ("we", "us") runs QuoteLeads Pay Per Lead, which connects homeowners who ask for a quote with one local business. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, where it's stored and your rights. We handle it under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. We never sell personal information.

1. Who this covers

Homeowners who make an enquiry through one of our forms or ads, and businesses (and their staff) who buy leads from us.

2. What we collect

From homeowners

The details you give when you ask for a quote: name, phone, email, postcode and suburb, and details about the job (for example homeowner status, power bill or timeline). We also collect your consent, including the name of the business you agreed could contact you, and when you gave it. If you reply to our texts, we keep those messages.

From businesses

Business name, legal name, ABN, contact name, email and phone, your service areas and lead preferences, your login, your account activity, and records of leads, charges, credits and invoices. Card details are collected and held by Stripe. We only see the card brand and last four digits.

Automatically

When you use the website or your account: IP address, browser and device type, and technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

3. How we use it

  • Homeowner enquiries: to check the enquiry is genuine (including a phone number check), find the business your consent covers, deliver your enquiry to that one business, and send follow-up texts on its behalf to help arrange a call.
  • Business accounts: to create and run your account, deliver leads, charge for them, issue tax invoices, handle credit claims, provide support and send service messages.
  • Running the service: preventing fraud and spam, fixing problems, keeping records required by law, and enforcing our Terms.

A homeowner's enquiry is only ever given to the business their consent covers. It isn't shared with other businesses.

5. Where it's stored

We're an Australian business, but the providers above store or process information overseas, in the countries listed. Under Australian Privacy Principle 8 we take reasonable steps to make sure they handle it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, and we remain accountable to you for it. Information held overseas may also be subject to the laws of that country.

6. Texts and opting out

If you made an enquiry, the business you chose (or we, on its behalf) may text you about it. Every text lets you opt out: reply STOP and you won't receive any more, or START to opt back in. We comply with the Spam Act 2003 and the Do Not Call Register Act 2006. Businesses that buy leads may only contact you about the enquiry you made.

7. Security

The website and accounts use encrypted connections. Our database providers encrypt stored data. Each business can only see the leads delivered to it, and only our authorised staff can see the rest. No system is completely secure, so keep your login details private and tell us at contact@quoteleads.com.au if you suspect a problem.

8. How long we keep it

We keep account and lead records while an account is open and for as long as we need them to run the service and resolve claims. Tax invoices and payment records are kept for as long as Australian tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, except what we must keep by law.

9. Cookies

This website doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers. Signing in stores a login session in your browser so you stay signed in. The installable app may store a notification subscription if you turn on push alerts.

10. Your rights and complaints

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or complain about how we've handled it, by emailing contact@quoteleads.com.au. We'll confirm who you are and respond within 30 days, usually at no charge. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

Businesses that receive a homeowner's details are responsible for handling them lawfully once delivered. Homeowners can also contact that business directly.

11. Data breaches

If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll contain it, tell the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires, and explain what happened and what to do.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version, and we'll email account holders about material changes. Questions: contact@quoteleads.com.au, QuoteLeads, ABN 83 894 208 171, Australia.
