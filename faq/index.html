Questions, answered

Billing, credits and delivery. The Terms have the full detail.

Billing

How am I charged?

Your first lead is paid in advance at sign-up and your card is saved. After that, each lead is charged to your card just before it's released to you, at its price plus GST. If you have account credit, it's used first.

Can I control how much I spend?

Yes. You set a weekly cap and we never deliver more leads than that in a week (Monday to Sunday). It's shown as a spending limit, for example "Weekly cap: 10 leads (max $1,155 inc GST)", so there are no surprise bills. You can change it or pause any time.

Is there a contract or minimum?

No contract, no minimum spend and no cancellation fee. Pause or stop whenever you like.

How do I cancel?

Whenever you like, with no fee and no notice period. Tap Pause delivery in your dashboard and no more leads are sent or charged. If you want your account closed completely, email us and we'll do it. You only ever pay for leads already delivered.

What if my card fails?

That lead isn't released to you and you aren't charged for it. Delivery pauses, and we email and text you a secure link to update your card. It restarts as soon as a new card is saved.

Do I get tax invoices?

Yes. A tax invoice for each week is emailed to you on Monday and can be downloaded from your account any time.

Leads

Are leads exclusive?

Yes. Each lead goes to one business only. Homeowners agree to be contacted by your business by name.

How are leads checked?

Before a lead reaches you, we check it has a real name, a working email and phone number, a postcode in your area, the service you selected, and isn't a duplicate. Leads that fail aren't sent or charged. What counts as a lead

How quickly will I get leads?

Leads arrive in real time, by email, SMS, your CRM (via webhook) and your account. How many you get depends on homeowner demand in your area. If your prepaid first lead hasn't arrived within 14 days, it's refunded.

Can you text leads for me?

Yes, if you leave automatic follow-up on. We text each new lead in your business name straight away, then on days 2, 5 and 10 until they reply, and replies aim to set up a call with you. You can switch it off any time.

Credits

What if a lead is bad?

Claim it from the lead in your account within 72 hours if it fails any of the lead criteria. Claims are checked automatically, and the credit is added straight away when our checks confirm it. Otherwise, or if you email us, we respond within 7 days.

What isn't covered?

The homeowner not answering, deciding not to go ahead, or getting other quotes.

How do credits work?

An approved claim adds the lead's price (ex GST) to your account credit, which is used automatically on your next leads before your card is charged.
